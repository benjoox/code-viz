# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming in issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces. Critique the work, explain your reasoning and respect different levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption
and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation for violations. They will consider context and severity and explain
moderation decisions where doing so does not expose private information or increase harm.
The same expectations apply to maintainers.

For private reports of abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
or the report control on the relevant content. Do not post private evidence in public issues.
For routine disagreements about a contribution, ask the maintainer for clarification in its thread.
